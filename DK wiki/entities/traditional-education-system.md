# Traditional Education System

**Type**: other

**Aliases**: Public School System, Centralized Institutions, Formal Education System, Decentralized Education, Schools, education systems

## Facts

- Aims to cultivate compliant and replaceable labor force
- Teaches convergent thinking with low efficiency and high cost
- Trust crisis exists due to failure to deliver safe future promises
- Aims to cultivate labor for employment systems.
- Contrasted with [[Creator Economy]] education models.
- Cannot keep up with new education methods.
- Personal responsibility and [[Self-Reliance]] are the true [[Goals]].
- [[Internet]] revolutionized education, making [[Creator]] new educators.
- Associated with the [[Modern Slavery]] of traditional work systems.
- held the most attention in the minds of children who grew into parents
- default outcome for the masses was slavery
- tell us what to learn, hinder our ability to think
