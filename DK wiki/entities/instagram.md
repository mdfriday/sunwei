# Instagram

**Type**: tool

**Aliases**: Social Media Platform, IG, Social Media

## Facts

- Used for tagging [[Creator]] and networking
- [[Social Media]] for sharing inspired posts with mutual benefit
- Used for medium form content (titles, Reels).
- Part of the top-down content creation system.
- Used for repurposing [[Twitter]] posts into carousel graphics
- Part of the [[Content Flywheel]] distribution strategy
- Post conversion target
- Used for testing ideas and building initial [[Potential Customer]] (0 to 200 fans)
- Short content [[Social Media]] for growth stage
- Used for carousel repurposing from long-form content
- Short form [[Social Media]] for attraction and [[Curiosity]]
- Used as a [[Social Media]] for [[Social Media]]/content distribution.
- Used for short content distribution and growth.
- [[Social Media]] for testing ideas and angles.
- Part of [[Internet]] front end for creating income.

## Connections

- [[Instagram]] related-to [[Non-Needy Networking]]: Platform used for tagging creators and executing the seven-step networking method.
