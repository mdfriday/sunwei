# LinkedIn

**Type**: other

**Aliases**: LI, Social Media Platform

## Facts

- Used for medium form content (posts).
- Part of the top-down content creation system.
- Used for [[Potential Customer]] development in traditional freelance mode
- Medium for repurposing [[Twitter]] posts into [[LinkedIn]] articles/posts
