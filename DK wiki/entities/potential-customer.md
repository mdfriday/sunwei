# Potential Customer

**Type**: other

**Aliases**: Client, Lead, Audience, Listeners, Target Audience

## Facts

- Acquired through personal brand trust rather than cold outreach
- Target of promotional activities integrated with content creation
- Target of content marketing funnels.
- Needs to be attracted by authority and relevance.
- Target of MVP offers (e.g., $500-$1000 starting price)
- Source of feedback for product iteration
- Content should focus on specific [[Potential Customer]] levels
- Modeled using the [[5 Levels of Consciousness]]
- Defined by persona, often the past self initially for research
- Pain points revolve around health, wealth, and relationships
- [[Potential Customer]] for Done For You services (freelance).
- [[Potential Customer]] for Done With You consulting.
- [[Potential Customer]] for Do It Yourself courses.

## Connections

- [[Potential Customer]] related-to [[Actionable Principles]]: Actionable Principles are used to attract Potential Customers by providing specific value and authority.
- [[Potential Customer]] related-to [[5 Levels of Consciousness]]: Content strategy must match the audience's current level in the 5 Levels of Consciousness model.
- [[Social Media]] related-to [[Potential Customer]]: Public content on social media attracts potential customers for Done For You or Do It Yourself products.
