# Universities

**Type**: other

**Aliases**: Educational Institutions, Prestigious Universities, Regular Education

## Facts

- Curriculum update speed is slow compared to the [[Internet]] era
- Courses often fail to keep pace with new education methods for personal revenue
- Limitations of traditional success path in the past (geography, prestige)
- Associated with [[Traditional Work Mode]] limitations
- Traditional education relies on them but trust is declining.
- People spend 4 to 12 years and thousands of dollars for entry-level jobs with low income.
- Knowledge needed for wealth cannot be learned in [[Universities]].

## Connections

- [[Universities]] related-to [[Education Gap]]: Slow curriculum updates in universities contribute to the disconnect between formal education and internet-era revenue methods.
