Six months ago, I lost my job. I expected stress, uncertainty, and maybe even panic. Instead, I discovered something I never anticipated: I feel happier, healthier, and more at peace than I have at any point in my adult life.  
  
In this vlog, I share the hidden costs of working that I didn't fully understand until I was forced into an involuntary early retirement. For decades, I accepted chronic stress, burnout, long commutes, poor sleep, unhealthy habits, and the constant pressure of corporate life as normal. Only after stepping away from work did I realize how much those invisible burdens were affecting my physical health, mental well-being, finances, relationships, and overall quality of life.  
  
This isn't an anti-work video. It's a reflection on what happens when the treadmill suddenly stops and you finally have the time and space to evaluate what work has been costing you all along.  
  
If you're dealing with a layoff, burnout, career anxiety, financial independence, early retirement, FIRE, or simply questioning the role work plays in your life, I hope this video helps.
