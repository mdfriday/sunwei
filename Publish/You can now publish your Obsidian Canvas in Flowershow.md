Hi! We've just shipped support for publishing **Obsidian Canvas** files in **Flowershow**.

If you’re using Canvas for visual notes, idea maps, or collecting linked material, you can now publish those too – directly from Obsidian via the **"Publish with Flowershow" plugin.**
