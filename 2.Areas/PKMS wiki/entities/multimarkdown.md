# MultiMarkdown

**Type**: tool

**Aliases**: multimarkdown/

## Facts

- Used for capturing ideas and [[Composing]] drafts.
- Has its own citations syntax.
- Easy to convert from .mmd to .tex.

## Connections

- [[MultiMarkdown]] related-to [[LaTeX]]: MultiMarkdown can be converted to LaTeX (.tex) and cleaned up afterwards using BibTeX format.
- [[Scrivener]] related-to [[MultiMarkdown]]: Scrivener utilizes MultiMarkdown, making it a fully compatible extension to the note-taking workflow.
