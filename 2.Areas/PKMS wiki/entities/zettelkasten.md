# Zettelkasten

**Type**: tool

**Aliases**: Zettelkasten Method, 卡片盒笔记法, Card box note-taking method

## Facts

- Used to [[Organize]] knowledge obtained and make sense of everything in the long term
- Helps track progress on [[Writing]] [[Projects]]
- German for 'card (Zettel) box (Kasten)'
- Created by [[Niklas Luhmann]]
- Digital version supported by [[Obsidian]], [[Logseq]], [[Heptabase]]
- [[Idea Verse Light]] for organizing notes to aid [[Writing]] and [[Thinking]]
- Stores fragments of text based on research completed
